# Upkeep, the build panel and status

## Upkeep loops (`ui/upkeep.py`, `ui/clan.py`, `ui/plates.py`)

- **`collect`** taps one marker of each resource, since a tap collects every collector of its kind; the markers are found by colour and then held to a size. A marker on the orange plate is over a full storage, which a tap cannot empty, so only the pale plate's are tapped; the dark marker is found by its icon on that plate, written down and learned per bubble from the gold and elixir markers on the same frame. Gains come from the storage bars, and a pass that finds the same markers standing stops (a full storage takes nothing).
- **`donate`** gives troops to whoever is asking. A tap gives immediately, which is what `--dry-run` is for.
- **`tap`, `swipe` and `confirm`** are the hands the agent upgrades through the build panel with: it reads every frame itself and picks the row, so the code names nothing and finds only the buttons it is about to press. They claim the emulator but go through `_attached` and never `_home`, so a panel left open stays open, and each saves the frame it left behind in its run directory for the agent to read. A sheet is told by its red X as well as its green button (`sheet_open`), since `upgrade_sheet` alone answers on any frame whose storages do not read, and each village draws its own: the builder base's 是否提升至N級？ is larger, with one button in the middle (bottom right on a hero's), white or red digits drawn bigger, a square coin and violet elixir, and pale enough to pass for a dialog, so the sheet is tested first. The button is the green patch the layout's price box lies on, not the biggest green in its span: a squad's research sheet stands a wider green banner above it, and green that lies only off the price is a sheet with nothing to price (`_sheet_button`). Under `--pay` it looks for the wall's menu several times before saying there is none (`WALL_MENU_LOOKS`), since the home village's translucent plates leave a single frame's price unread. `confirm` presses once (under `--pay`, the wall's price button and then what that raised) and then looks several times, cancelling a dialog that slides in, so it never presses the same thing twice.
- **`boost`** starts the builder base clock tower's free boost (`clock_tower_marker`, `free_boost`, `boost_sheet`). It parks the camera first (`selected` with something selected, `never_settled` on a park that fell short), since the game leaves it wherever it was, by the dock with the tower off screen after a crossing. Out of turn the same button asks for gems, so it presses nothing without the marker the game floats over the tower only while the free boost is ready, taps the tower below it, and presses only the button carrying the tower's clock with no price printed on it and no blue or green plate, once. That press raises the boost's own sheet, whose lime 加速 it presses because it raised that sheet itself, as `confirm` does a dialog; nothing on the sheet tells a gem, the gem icon being the same yellow-green. It leaves the tower selected for the agent to clear.
- **`helper`** sends the Builder's Apprentice (`apprentice`) or the Lab Assistant (`assistant`) to a running home village job the agent names by the game's name (`send`, ticking 將被指派直到升級完成 only under `--recurring`), or stops the recurring dispatch it is on (`cancel`); which job, and when to stop, is the agent's call. Its arguments name an action and a helper, so the hut's other two helpers, which convert resources rather than speed a job, can come in later as actions of their own. Nothing here reads text, so the row is found by its place: the Helper Hut lists the slot's running jobs by time left, longest first, which is the order of the export's timers, so it reads the export first (`_exported`, which also says whether the helper can be sent and the job is running), opens the hut off the plate the build panel floats beside the open tab (`HELPER_PLATE`), and presses 指派 only where the list's whole buttons line up with the export's jobs (`helper_rows`, scrolled to the end for a job past the fourth). 將被指派直到升級完成 is pressed only where it does not read as asked, since the game keeps it as each helper last had it, and 確認 is pressed once. `cancel` taps the helper's face on the recurring job's row (`HUT_RECURRING_X`: first while the helper works on it, which the hut pins to the top, else its place in the list) and presses 確認 on the two-button dialog that tap raised (`helper_stop_prompt`, which reads as the wall dialogs do); the hour under way still finishes there and the cooldown runs, so a change of job takes effect at the next round. **The hut opens only off its plate** (`helper_plate`, read before the tap), which floats while the helper can be sent or works and not while it cools down, when the point is the map: so both actions answer `no_hut` through a cooldown, and `watch --helpers` wakes at every helper's cooldown end, a recurring one's included, since until it goes back is the window to change its dispatch. **`sent` and `cancelled` rest on the export read afterwards**, the row the helper now works on (`helper_timer`) and whether a running job of its slot still carries `helper_recurrent`, since the dialogs name the job only in text. The dialog, the hut and the panel are closed by their own X on every path, and `tended` lists the `sent` and `cancelled` runs.
- **`prospector`** converts hero equipment ore with the Prospector, the hut's fourth helper, at the slider's maximum, from the ore the agent names into the one it names, or with no ores reads the ores and their caps alone; which ores is the agent's call. It reads the export first, and a conversion opens nothing while the Prospector cools down (`cooling`), since the plate it opens off floats only while it is free; a read of the ores needs no Prospector. The ores are read off the build panel's hero equipment tab, whose bars are the storages' slid left with the panel (`ore_stock`, `ORE_SHIFT`), and each cap off its bar's tooltip on every run (`ore_capacity`, a second tap on the bar shuts it), since the caps rise with the village. The window keeps its reels as they were last left, so each is read off its centred ore's colour and stepped one ore per drag until both read the ores asked (`reel_ore`, `_turn_reels`). **Spending is guarded twice**: 轉換 is pressed once, only on reels reading the ores asked and a green button (`convert_offered`), and it raises 要轉換資源嗎？, whose 確認 is pressed once, only when that dialog names the same two ores (`convert_prompt`). **`converted` rests on the export read afterwards**, the Prospector's `helper_cooldown`; `after` is the bars read again. `status` and `watch --helpers` time it beside the two helpers (`PROSPECTOR`), and `tended` lists the `converted` runs.
- **`stock`** reports what the village on screen holds as a share of its ceilings. It reports whichever village is up and never crosses.
- **`status`** reads the counters in the bottom right on either village (`plate_count`, `parsers/home.py`) without opening the panel, plus the stock and the shield bar (無 or its countdown), and takes its timers from the village export (`_exported`, the walk `export` uses, run only on a village), worked through the running speed-ups (`parsers/village.py`: `SPEED_UPS`, measured rates). So it covers both villages without sailing and asks no model. It prints the report `takeover` and `upgrades` hand the user (`status_text`: each village's line, then its upgrades as a table of facts: when, whose slot with a goblin's marked, and the item with the level being bought over its `max_lvl` and the `limited_lvl` where it stops short, then what its next level asks and how long it takes, `—` where it cannot continue; what follows a row is the agent's to decide and add, never the table's), `--json` the model; `--last` reads the newest saved export instead and touches nothing, and `--within` folds rows past that many hours into one line. The next level's price and time are the list's times the shares the village's newest purchases within `DISCOUNT_FRESH` paid against them (`_paid_shares`, off the journal's `confirm` lines; the time's off the first `status` after the press that lists the job, `_time_share`, floored as the game charges, `_panel_floor`), which the head line names. A `listprice` line ends them on its village, and the agent writes one when the panel shows a price with no struck-through list price under it. The report keeps `next` at list and the shares in `paid` and `timed`. Under the village whose storages were read come a `資源不足` line per resource they do not cover if every row due within `SHORT_WITHIN` hours continued (a row that cannot continue adds nothing; one the agent will switch adds its own next level, since only the agent knows what goes there), then the speed-ups still running and when each ends. Each village's block ends with the user's notes for it (`notes`, the state file's, `--last` included), as written and numbered as `note` numbers them, and the report's last line is their revision, which `note add` and `note done` take; like the rows, they are facts, never applied to a row. The head line dates the export (`_dated`), so a report pasted later still says which day its rows' 今天 and 明天 mean. The panels draw a countdown at normal speed whatever is running, which is why they are not the source: under a builder potion one read 6小時 for a job done 40 minutes later. A Builder's Apprentice or Lab Assistant working on a job is counted for what is left of its work there (`helper_timer`), at the multiplier its level has in the game's helper table, and the row names it and when that work ends; its return to the job after its cooldown (`helper_recurrent`) cannot be timed and is not predicted. A helper with no recurring job is a row of the home village's table like the clock tower's, at the time it can next be sent (`helper_cooldowns`); one that recurs has no row, since a helper speeds one job, and that job (the running one of its slot carrying `helper_recurrent`, `recurrent`) names it in its slot cell between its rounds. Sending one or stopping its recurrence is `helper`'s. **The shield is not watched**: the user has said more than once that its time does not matter, since loot is cheap and a few battles win back what a raid takes (2026-10-10, 2026-10-13), so nothing sleeps on it or sails for it. Its countdown is read since 2026-10-14, when he asked for it once a shield was up to measure; 天 and 秒 on the bar have not been seen and read as None.

**A price is what the menu says today, not a fixed table.** The gold pass and event tasks discount upgrades on every building, walls and heroes included, so the same level can ask 8 500 000 one week and 8 000 000 the next; a price that differs from an earlier run or the wiki is not a misread. So nothing, in the code or in an agent's reasoning, assumes what a building, a wall or a hero costs (`MIN_PRICE` is only a floor against single figures), and a suspected misread is settled against a crop of the button, never against a remembered price. `status`'s next-level price and time are the one estimate: the list's scaled by what the village's newest purchases paid, labelled with those purchases, for deciding whether to farm and when to wake; nothing spends on it. `search`'s totals to an item's cap (`LevelsTotal`, each level of the list summed) carry the same estimate beside them, each level scaled and floored before the sum, for builder, laboratory and pet items only, since the shares come from their purchases. A price guessed too low has the takeover skip a farm run while the upgrade waits, so a discount stops counting after `DISCOUNT_FRESH` or at a `listprice` line, and a time share the price's does not explain is taken at its low end.

Prices in a building row shrink with their length, so `digits_from` takes the row floor as a parameter. A truncated price is the dangerous misread, since seven of eight digits still clears `MIN_PRICE`.
